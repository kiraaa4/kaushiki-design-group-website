Twenty Five plus years of building in Telangana

Kaushiki Design Group was founded in Hyderabad in 2001 by a structural engineer who wanted design and construction under one roof. Today our team of architects, structural engineers and site supervisors handles projects from the first sketch to the final handover.

We keep a small number of live projects at a time so every site gets a weekly visit from a senior architect.
